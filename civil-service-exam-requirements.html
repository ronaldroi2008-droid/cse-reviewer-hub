Civil Service Exam Requirements 2026: Complete Application Guide for Filipino Examinees

The Civil Service Examination (CSE) is one of the most important qualifying examinations in the Philippines. Passing the exam grants Civil Service Eligibility, which is commonly required for permanent positions in government agencies and offices.

Before filing an application, every examinee should understand the Civil Service Exam requirements to avoid delays, rejection of application, or missing important deadlines.

Who Can Take the Civil Service Exam?

Applicants must meet the following qualifications set by the Civil Service Commission (CSC):

  • Must be a Filipino citizen.
  • Must be at least 18 years old on the date of filing the application.
  • Must be of good moral character.
  • Must not have been convicted of a crime involving moral turpitude.
  • Must not have been dishonorably discharged from military service.
  • Must not have been dismissed from government service for cause.
  • Must not have taken the same level of Civil Service Examination within three months before the examination date.

Civil Service Exam Requirements 2026

Applicants are generally required to submit the following documentary requirements personally:

1. Accomplished Application Form

Applicants must complete and submit the official Civil Service Exam Application Form (CS Form No. 100). The form can be downloaded directly from the official Civil Service Commission website.

2. Four Passport-Sized ID Pictures

Prepare four identical passport-sized photographs with a plain white background. Photos must comply strictly with the CSC photo requirements and specifications (must include a comprehensive, handwritten name tag with signature).

3. Valid Identification Card

Applicants must present the original and a photocopy of a valid ID. Accepted IDs include:

  • Philippine Passport
  • Driver's License
  • PhilSys National ID
  • Voter's ID
  • School ID or Company ID
  • Other CSC-accepted government-issued IDs

Note: The ID must contain the applicant's clear name, photo, date of birth, and signature whenever applicable.

4. Examination Fee

Applicants must pay the examination fee upon submission of their application. The current CSE-PPT (Pen and Paper Test) examination fee is ₱500.

5. Additional Requirements for Dual Citizens

Applicants with dual citizenship are required to submit an Identification Certificate issued by the Bureau of Immigration.

How to Apply for the Civil Service Exam

  1. Download the Application Form: Obtain the official CS Form No. 100 from the Civil Service Commission.
  2. Prepare Your Documentary Requirements: Gather your accomplished application form, passport-sized photos, valid ID, and the exact examination fee.
  3. Visit the CSC Office: Submit your application personally at the nearest CSC Regional Office or Field Office during the official filing period. Applications are usually accepted on a first-come, first-served basis.
  4. Receive Your Application Receipt: Keep your application receipt and examinee number safe. These will be needed when checking examination results and rooms.

Important Reminders

  • Apply as early as possible because some testing centers may close applications ahead of time once they reach maximum capacity.
  • Verify all information and ensure no blanks are left on your CS Form 100 before submission.
  • Always bring original documents for verification when filing your application.
  • Follow the latest official CSC announcements regarding examination schedules and testing centers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can High School Graduates Take the Civil Service Exam?
Yes. Educational attainment is generally not a requirement for the Career Service Examination – Pen and Paper Test (CSE-PPT), provided the applicant meets the basic age and citizenship requirements.
How Much Is the Civil Service Exam Fee?
The examination fee for the regular CSE-PPT is ₱500.
Where Can I Download the Civil Service Exam Application Form?
The official application form is completely free and available through the Civil Service Commission website under the downloadable CSC forms section.

Final Thoughts

Preparing your Civil Service Exam requirements early can help ensure a smooth, hassle-free application process. Once your requirements are complete and submitted, you can shift your full focus to reviewing and preparing for the examination itself.
